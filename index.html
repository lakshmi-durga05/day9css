About Ravi Teja

Bhupatiraju Ravi Shankar (born 26 January 1968), known professionally as Ravi Teja, is an Indian actor and film producer known for his work in Telugu cinema. Known for his comic timing, he has received several accolades, including three Nandi Awards and a Filmfare Award South. He is popularly referred to as "Mass Maharaja".

Teja began his career with an uncredited role in Karthavyam (1990) and went on to play minor roles in several films. He made his debut as a lead actor with Nee Kosam (1999), which earned him the Nandi Special Jury Award.

Teja gained recognition with Itlu Sravani Subramanyam (2001), Aunu Valliddaru Ishta Paddaru! (2002), and Idiot (2002), establishing himself as a leading actor.
